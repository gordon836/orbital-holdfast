Orbital Holdfast

Nobody saw what did it. One night the sky went white without a sound, and when it faded, the world was gone.

What's left

The hall. A few streets. The hill behind the school. Floating in the dark with the wreck of the planet turning slowly around it.

The rocks falling on you are pieces of home. Some of them carry a purple crystal nobody has seen before.

And when the sky passes over, it pulls. Loose metal lifts off the rock and goes up into the dark — the crews call it the tide. Anything dug into bedrock holds. Guns don't. Old Tam keeps the patterns, and the crews build them again every time.

Five of you

You are Ren. You fix things. People are looking at you.

During the waves you build guns only — mines, farms, homes and the rest are raised at the Home Base between runs. Tap a card, then a cell, to build. Tap the ground to send Ren; tap a building to post her, repair it, or upgrade it; tap a rock to make it her target. Reach the escape pods before the wave ends. Drag to spin and tilt, pinch to zoom. Survive ten waves.
