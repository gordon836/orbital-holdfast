Privacy Policy — Orbital Holdfast

Effective 4 September 2026

Orbital Holdfast does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.

It contains no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before any new version is released.
